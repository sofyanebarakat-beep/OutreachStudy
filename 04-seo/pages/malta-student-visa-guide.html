Planning to study in Malta? This practical guide explains the main student visa and residence steps for international students, the documents commonly requested, and when to begin.

Important: Requirements depend on your nationality, course level and duration. Always confirm your case with Identità Malta and your licensed school before applying.

Do I need a student visa for Malta?

EU, EEA and Swiss citizens do not need an entry visa, although students staying longer than three months may need to register their residence. Most non-EU students taking a course lasting more than 105 days must obtain a national long-stay study visa before travelling. Shorter courses follow different entry rules.

Malta student visa process

  1. Choose an accredited programme and receive your acceptance letter.
  2. Check the rules for your nationality and course duration.
  3. Prepare the required financial, accommodation, insurance and academic documents.
  4. Submit your application within the official timeframe.
  5. After arrival, complete any residence-permit steps that apply to your programme.

Documents commonly requested

  • Valid passport and passport copies
  • School acceptance or enrolment letter
  • Proof of tuition payment where required
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Accommodation confirmation
  • Travel and medical insurance
  • Flight itinerary and supporting civil documents where applicable

Documents may need official English translations or legalisation. Do not submit an application using an old checklist.

When should I apply?

Identità advises eligible study-visa applicants to submit at least eight weeks before the course begins and permits applications up to six months before the start date. Starting early gives you time to correct missing documents.

Student residence permits

Students on longer courses may also need a residence permit after arriving in Malta. The exact route differs for programmes below MQF Level 5 and higher-education programmes at MQF Level 5 or above.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for a Malta student visa without an acceptance letter?

An acceptance or enrolment letter from the education provider is normally a core application document.

Can I work while studying in Malta?

Some non-EU students may work under specific conditions and with the required employment authorisation. Check the current rules for your visa and permit.

Does Outreach Study guarantee visa approval?

No. We provide programme and application guidance, but only the Maltese authorities decide visa and residence applications.
