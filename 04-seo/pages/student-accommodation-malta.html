Finding student accommodation in Malta is easier when you compare location, total monthly cost and contract terms—not rent alone. This guide helps international students choose a practical place to live before their course begins.

Student accommodation options in Malta

Shared apartments

Sharing an apartment is a common way to reduce costs and meet other students. Confirm how many people live in the property and whether bills are included.

Private rooms and studios

A private room offers more independence, while a studio gives you your own living space. These options usually cost more than a shared room.

School or student residences

Some schools work with student residences or accommodation providers. This can simplify arrival, but availability may be limited during busy periods.

Host families

Living with a host family can provide a structured environment and more opportunities to practise English in daily life.

Where should students live in Malta?

Choose an area based on your school, public transport and daily needs. Popular student areas include Msida, Gżira, Sliema, St Julian’s and locations close to major education centres. A cheaper room can become expensive if the commute is long or unreliable.

Accommodation checklist

  • Walking and public-transport time to school
  • Rent, deposit and agency fees
  • Electricity, water and internet costs
  • Number of occupants and shared facilities
  • Contract length and cancellation rules
  • Inventory, condition report and deposit receipt
  • Heating, cooling, laundry and kitchen access

Avoid accommodation scams

Do not transfer money based only on social-media messages. Verify the property, identity of the landlord or agent, written agreement, payment terms and address. Keep receipts and copies of all correspondence.

Accommodation documents for visa applications

Visa applicants may need evidence showing where they will stay. Confirm the exact format and coverage period required for your application with Identità Malta or the relevant visa centre before paying a non-refundable deposit.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book accommodation?

Begin researching as soon as your study dates are confirmed. Availability and prices can change considerably during busy periods.

Are utility bills included in student rent?

Sometimes, but not always. Ask for the expected total monthly cost and how electricity and water usage are calculated.

Can Outreach Study guarantee accommodation?

No. We can provide planning guidance, but students should verify the provider, contract and property before paying.
